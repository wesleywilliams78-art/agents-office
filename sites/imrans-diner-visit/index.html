How to run the day
  • Open a block: read the purpose, tick the steps as you go, ask the questions and type or dictate the answer under each.
  • Show me: ask to see the thing itself. Mark it Shown, Partly or Could not show. What cannot be shown is evidence too.
  • "What the manual says" holds the written standard for that block, with its chapter. Score against it: Matches, Differs, or No standard yet. With no written standard, use No standard yet, not Needs work.
  • Log a pain point the moment you find one: what hurts, the evidence, who feels it, how bad and how often. The Report ranks them.
  • T13 and T14 are the evening: prep as the second shift arrives, then the live service as the trade builds. T11 conversations can be spread through the day.
  • Use role titles, not names. Nothing is shared on site.
Where the manual contradicts itself

The manual's cover says "working draft, pending review", and the SOPs are drafts for internal review. Ask which version the team works to, and log a pain point where a contradiction causes one.
