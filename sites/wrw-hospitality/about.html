Who I am

I’m Wesley Williams. I work with multi-site hospitality and franchise operators on the part between a good first site and a working estate.

I’ve spent more than fifteen years in multi-site hospitality and franchising, and I came up through the floor: assistant manager, general manager, then regional operations across the UK and Germany.

Most recently I was head of operations for a fully franchised estate: £40M under my P&L, ten country strategies developed and an operating model built from the ground up. Every site’s profit could be seen for the first time, every site answered to one scorecard, and each new opening became repeatable rather than one-off.
