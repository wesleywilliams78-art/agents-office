# The Franchise Entry Report

A country-by-country report for brands thinking of franchising into a new market: where the opportunity is, what blocks it, and what to do in the first 90 days. **One country every Friday**, on the WRW Hospitality LinkedIn page, reposted by Wesley. **The Tuesday after, a motion recap** of that country (see `recap/` in each issue). It sits under **WRW Hospitality**, next to Art of Operations and The Operator's Ledger.

## The standard (from Issue 01)
Every issue keeps the same seven pages, so readers can compare countries:
1. **Cover:** country, issue number and date, the verdict in two sentences, three headline numbers.
2. **Market snapshot:** population, GDP per head, food-service spend, franchise count, consumer spending, currency; then how the country eats out.
3. **What's rising, what's falling:** category by category, with evidence, source, window, confidence, saturation and ease of entry.
4. **White-space opportunities:** up to four gaps sized S, M or L, and Wesley's "If I had a brand" view.
5. **Regulation and cost to operate:** franchise law, trade mark, food safety, alcohol, halal, tax, company set-up, and the true cost of labour.
6. **Entry route and risks:** master franchise, area developer, direct, company-owned, with real examples; six sourced risks.
7. **The first 90 days:** ten moves in order, what we'd check next, the method and the numbered source list.

**Rules:**
- Public sources only, every figure dated and coded to the source list.
- A rise or fall call needs two independent signals. If there's only one, the row says **Thin**. Missing data says **Not found**, never a guess.
- Estimates are labelled with their method.
- Money is shown in the local currency with the £ conversion and its date.
- "Not legal, tax or investment advice." on the regulation page and in the method.
- No client is ever named. YSF Advisory never appears.
- From Issue 02: the © line "© 2026 WRW Hospitality (Wesley Williams). Not to be resold, rebranded or redistributed." on the cover or the last page.
- The last page asks "Which country should we cover next?" and invites people to follow WRW Hospitality.
- WRW Hospitality only: never the Scaling Without Borders label (from Issue 02).
- One country colour per issue, from the flag (from Issue 02; see below).

## Country colour (from Issue 02; Wesley, 24 Oct 2026)
Each issue takes one colour from the country's flag, so the series varies week to week while staying recognisably WRW.
- **What changes:** the country colour replaces WRW red on the cover field, the closing band, the "If I had a brand" box, section labels, rules, hero numbers and the one highlighted series in each chart. A second flag colour may be used for thin rules only.
- **What never changes:** the seven-page layout, the type, the bone pages and ink text, the footer, the mountain line, and the WRW logo. The logo tile is white on red (`#FF3131`) only when the issue is red; on any other country colour the tile takes the report colour with white "WRW" text (outlined in white on the cover), so red never clashes with it (Wesley, 24 Oct 2026).
- **Red flags use WRW red.** If the flag's main colour is red (Norway, Denmark, Poland, Switzerland, Austria), the issue uses `#FF3131`.
- **Readable first.** The cover field must hold white text at least as well as WRW red does: a contrast of 3.6:1 or better. If the flag colour can't (yellow, orange, light green), use its darker flag colour, or set the cover text in ink.
- **Colours only, never the flag.** No flag graphic, emblem, coat of arms or text from a flag. Some flags carry religious text (Saudi Arabia) and some countries restrict commercial use of national symbols.
- **Colour never carries meaning.** Rise and fall keep their arrows and words; other data stays ink and grey.
- **Record it.** Each issue's `ISSUE.md` notes its colour and contrast, and it's added to the palette table below. The explainer video uses the same colour.

## Colour and spacing rules added 24 Oct 2026
- **Rising is green, falling is red.** Arrows and the unit-count chart use green (#1E9E57) for up and red (#D8362B) for down; mixed, flat and Not found stay neutral grey.
- **Spacing is automatic.** Each page raises its spacing until the page is full and tightens it, then the type, only if it would overflow. The copyright line sits above the closing band, clear of the mountains.
- **Build it:** `node connectors/franchise-report/build2.mjs issue.json out.pdf`. The office builds the same way from the ISSUE JSON block (see `ferfile.mjs`).

## Secondary flag colour on the cover (Norway, approved 25 Oct 2026; every issue from now on)
- The cover rules (the line under the subtitle and the three above the stats) and the mountain lines on the cover and on the closing band are NOT white. They use the country's second flag colour, in three shades: **front** (rules and the bold front ridge), **mid** (the low bottom wave), **back** (the faint back ridge). Lines are solid, not see-through.
- Norway: flag blue on the red report. front #00205B, mid #2F6FD0, back #9CC7F5.
- Sweden: flag yellow on the blue report. front #FECC02, mid #D9A600, back #FFE88A.
- Denmark (red) and Finland (blue) have white as their second flag colour, so their rules and mountains stay white.
- For any other country, take the second flag colour and make the three shades the same way (strong, mid, light), checking they read against the report colour. The builder (`connectors/franchise-report/build2.mjs`) holds the table and takes an `accent` object in the issue JSON to override it.

## Fixing an issue from the office (2 Nov 2026)
Desks correct a delivered issue with `franchise_report_edit`: field edits on a copy of `issue.json`. The tool checks for doubled text, the bar and the page fit, builds `revisions/<time>-edit/`, and ends with a `REVISION READY:` line. When Wesley approves the card, the server (`ferfile.promote`) makes that revision the live issue and keeps the old files in `revisions/<time>-replaced/`. Nothing is posted or copied to Drive.
