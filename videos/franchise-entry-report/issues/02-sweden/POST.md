**Wesley's repost (his profile; his own draft, tightened 30 Oct 2026):**

Sweden charges half the VAT on takeaway food that it does on eating in. 6% against 12%, until the end of 2027. 🏆

This week's Franchise Entry Report from WRW Hospitality takes a seat in Sweden. Chicken is still an attractive gap, but the tax setup opens a different route to scale: build around takeaway.

Labour helps too. Of the three Nordic markets we've covered, Sweden is the cheapest to staff: roughly 15 to 25% less per hour, all-in, than Norway or Denmark.

If you're thinking of taking a food brand abroad, that's a window with an end date.

Catch the FREE report below, and follow WRW Hospitality for a new country every Friday ⤵️
