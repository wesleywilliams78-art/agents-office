## The verdict
Open, and cheaper than Norway, but crowded at the top. Burgers are taken, pizza and café chains are failing, and the live window is takeaway-led fast food while takeaway VAT stays at 6% to the end of 2027.

## Headline numbers
- 10.62m people (Aug 2026), growth 0.15% a year
- Restaurant and hotel prices 23% above the EU average (Norway 40%)
- VAT on takeaway food 6%, against 12% eating in, to 31 Dec 2027
