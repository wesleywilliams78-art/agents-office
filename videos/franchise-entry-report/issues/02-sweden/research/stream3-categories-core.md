# Sweden, Stream 3: Category movement (part 1) - Chicken, Burger, Pizza, Kebab/grill, Coffee

Researched Sat 24 Oct 2026. Public sources only. Every number below was read in the page or PDF named, except those marked **[snippet]** (search-result summary only, not opened: open before publishing).

**Method notes**
- COUNT = made by me from a locator I opened. Only two are of that kind: Domino's (15, dominos.se/butiker, 24 Oct 2026, counted from the 15 store records embedded in the page) and Holy Kebab (8, holykebab.se list, 24 Oct 2026). Everything else is a company-reported figure (annual or sustainability report = High), press (Medium) or a brand claim (Thin).
- KFC, Burger King, MAX, Espresso House, Bastard Burgers and Wayne's locators are JavaScript maps or app shells, or sit behind a cookie-consent wall. I did not click "allow all cookies" to force them. So no locator counts for them. Not found by me, not necessarily unavailable.
- Date traps: some Realtid pages show the site's current date (24 Oct 2026) at the top but the article is old (Sibylla "mer korv" = 27 Apr 2010; NSP "burgarbolag" = 25 Aug 2010). Search engines surfaced these as "2026". I only use dates read inside the article text.

- Entry difficulty: **High**. Two US master brands have failed to reach scale or survive. Evidence of cause is the operators' own statements (profitability, franchisor dispute), not a market study.
