# Issue 02 Sweden: Stream 4. Category movement part 2, entry routes, risks

Researched 24 Oct 2026 for the 30 Oct 2026 issue. Public sources only. Every figure below was read on the page or PDF named, except lines marked **[snippet]**, which come from a search-result summary and were not opened; treat those as leads, not facts.

Confidence key: High = locator count, annual/sustainability report, regulatory filing or official statistics. Medium = reputable press. Thin = single weak source. A rise/fall call needs two independent signals, otherwise it is Thin.

Read before using: several Swedish news sites (Realtid, Affärsvärlden) show the reader's current date, or a wrong date, on old articles. ## A. Category movement

### Segment backdrop (Visita Restaurangindex, High)

Visita, Restaurangindex andra kvartalet 2026, dated 15 Sep 2026, https://visita.se/app/uploads/2026/09/Restaurangindex-kvartal-2-2026-inkl.-omslag.pdf (downloaded and read):
- Total restaurant sales Q2 2026 +6.1% value, +2.8% volume vs Q2 2025 (50,823 vs 47,913 MSEK incl. VAT).
- Q2 2026 volume by segment: fast food +6.3%; hotel restaurants +5.4%; cafés/konditorier +5.0%; traffic-adjacent ("trafiknära") +5.0%; lunch and evening restaurants +1.0%; pubs/bars/entertainment +0.1%.
- Q1-Q2 2026 volume: fast food +5.6%; cafés +2.1%; traffic-adjacent +1.7%; lunch/evening -0.8%.
- Market still 1.2% below its Q2 2022 volume peak. Visita says lunch/evening restaurants are the types most disadvantaged by the 1 Apr 2026 food VAT change, with substitution towards home, ready meals and takeaway.

4. Fuel tax cut: temporary energy tax cut on petrol and diesel from 1 May 2026, extended proposals to 30 Nov 2026 **[snippet]**, regeringen.se/Drivkraft Sverige consultation responses; not confirmed as enacted. Thin.

## B. Entry routes (Swedish examples)

### B1. Master franchise
- Burger King: King Food Sverige AB is the master franchisee, with a franchisee network ("over 100 restaurants", company claim, undated). https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/burger-king/search. History: NSP bought 21 Burger King restaurants from Burger King Corporation in 2004, 7 in Denmark 2006, 9 in southern Sweden 2007, and ran 60+ by 2015 (Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordic_Service_Partners). Which entity runs how many today is not verified.
- KFC via NSP: agreement with Yum! signed 2014, first restaurant Lockarp 2015, 24 in 2025 (same Wikipedia page). About 10 years to 24 units. Thin.
- Subway: development agreement with Nordic Bites for Finland and Sweden from 2010; full master franchise for four countries 20 May 2025; 250 new restaurants targeted by 2032; Juuri Partners (PE) minority since Jan 2023. Subway newsroom link above. High.
- Domino's: 2016 Domino's Pizza Group took a 45% stake in each of the Norwegian and Swedish businesses (Iceland master franchisee as vehicle), first store Malmö Dec 2016 (QSR Magazine / franchising.com **[snippet]**). Sold PPS Foods (Domino's Sweden, 14 stores, master franchisee) to Eyja fjárfestingafélag III, controlled by Birgir Bieltvedt, for EUR 2m cash, completed 2 May 2021 (announced 9 Mar 2021, IFA Magazine https://ifamagazine.com/dominos-sells-sweden-unit-for-2m/; completion per Domino's RNS **[snippet]**). Part of the group's exit from directly operated international markets. This was a divestment, not a bankruptcy. Whether the brand still trades in Sweden under the buyer was not checked.
- Starbucks: licence deal with ICA Sept 2014 (**[snippet]**, ICA press release); first Swedish café 2010; peak 17 cafés; Norwegian Dely took over Sweden in summer 2021; two cafés left Oct 2021 (Dagens PS above).
- Taco Bell: franchisee TB Group Scandinavia, first store 27 Nov 2025 (above).
- Five Guys: operated through Five Guys Scandinavia AB (job-ad and TT sources). Whether this is a master franchise or company-owned arm of Five Guys' European owner was **not verified**. Treat as unverified.
- Control Low-Medium (brand owner sets standards; master owns local rights). Capital High for the master (must fund roll-out or sub-franchise); Low for the brand. Speed Medium-High if the master has capital, slow if not: KFC 24 units in ~10 years, Domino's 14 in ~4.5 years then exit, Pizza Hut 17 (2016) to 25 (2025) then bankruptcy.

### B2. Area developer
- Closest Swedish example: Subway's 2010 development agreement with Nordic Bites (Finland and Sweden), which became the four-country master franchise in 2025 with a 250-unit target. A development schedule that later converts to a master is the pattern. Subway newsroom link above.
- No other dated Swedish area-developer agreement found.
- Control Medium (schedule enforced by franchisor). Capital Medium (developer funds sites). Speed Medium. Thin; one example.

### B3. Direct franchise (domestic chains growing this way)
- Pinchos: opened to franchisees (2014 per **[snippet]**), "more than 80% franchisee-run" (company claim, https://pinchos.se/franchise); over 60 Swedish restaurants (EFN 28 Aug 2026); PE-owned (Valedo since 2018) and for sale.
- Sibylla: ~120 franchise outlets, franchisor owned by franchisees' jointly held company until sold for SEK 1 to Burgsvik (3 Sep 2026).
- Wayne's Coffee: franchise chain, franchisor bankrupt 4 Jul 2026, brand and franchisees to continue under another company.
- McDonald's Sweden: 24 franchisees (21 Nov 2025). Franchising page says one restaurant (equipment, interior, signage) typically costs SEK 15-25m **[snippet]**, page returned 403.
- Control High for franchisor, Capital Low for franchisor and franchisee-funded, Speed High where unit economics are proven (Pinchos from 2014 to 60+ in about 12 years). Evidence of franchisor fragility: Wayne's and Sibylla franchisor transfers.

### B4. Company-owned launches
- MAX Burgers: owns 199 of 203 restaurants, 163 in Sweden, only four franchises (all in Sweden). Self-funded from a family-owned base (91% family-owned). FY2025 report above. Turnover growth +2% in 2025 despite 8 new restaurants, so like-for-like was flat or down (derived; the report gives no LFL figure).
- Brödernas: company-owned and rapidly expanded, then reconstruction Oct 2024 with ~70-75 restaurants and a loss of 175 MSEK in 2023; 33 subsidiaries bankrupt by Mar 2025. Shows the downside of owning leases.
- Five Guys (if company/JV-owned): three units in about 2 years (2024 to Apr 2026).
- Control Very High. Capital Very High. Speed Low-Medium (Five Guys 3 units in ~2 years; MAX 8 a year).

### B5. Failed or exited entries
- Domino's: entered Dec 2016, parent sold the Swedish business 2 May 2021 for EUR 2m, 14 stores.
- Pizza Hut: franchisee NRG Pizza reconstruction Nov 2025 (25 restaurants), bankruptcy 9 Sep 2026 (18 closed, ~250 staff). Prisma Properties said NRG rented eight of its properties (Örebro, Västerås, Sundsvall, Norrköping, Karlstad, Handen, Norsborg, Växjö) (EFN, https://efn.se/pizza-hut-konkursen-slar-mot-prisma **[snippet]**). Global brand owner Long Range Capital since summer 2025 per Dagens PS **[snippet]**.
- Starbucks: from 17 cafés to 2 (by Oct 2021).
- Wayne's Coffee: 90 (2017) to 58 (Jul 2026), franchisor bankrupt.
- Vapiano: reconstruction Jan 2025.
- Brödernas, Klang Market: see A3.
- "Wayne's" as an international exit: World Coffee Portal says it "exited international markets" and closed Norwegian cafés (Affärsvärlden **[snippet]**, undated).

## C. Risks

### C1. Costs and demand
- Restaurant prices +2.7% over 12 months to Aug 2026, contributing 0.2 points to CPI; CPI 0.3%, KPIF 0.7% in Aug 2026. SCB, 14 Sep 2026, https://www.scb.se/hitta-statistik/statistik-efter-amne/priser-och-ekonomiska-tendenser/priser/konsumentprisindex-kpi/pong/statistiknyhet/konsumentprisindex-kpi-augusti-2026/ (opened). High. SCB's food price fall of -6.6% y/y Aug 2026 is from a search summary only **[snippet]** and is mostly the mechanical effect of the 12% to 6% food VAT cut.
- Margins: Visita (Sep 2025): "profitability is strained as purchase prices have risen more than sales prices; bankruptcies strongly elevated". Volume down five quarters in a row to Q2 2025; still 1.2% below the Q2 2022 volume peak in Q2 2026 (Visita index reports above). High.
- Visita government submission (undated): since early 2022 restaurant food purchase prices +38% vs menu prices +22% **[snippet]**, https://www.regeringen.se/contentassets/adbdac44539d41fc8ac039994180a35f/visita.pdf. Thin until opened.
- Staff cost share: the median restaurant company spends about 36% of turnover on personnel, three times the grocery sector. Visita/Makrologik report "När momsen styr måltiden", May 2026, https://visita.se/app/uploads/2026/06/Momsrapporten-260622.pdf (downloaded, read). High (commissioned by Visita, an interested party).
- Demand mix Q2 2026: fast food +6.3%, lunch/evening +1.0% (Visita index). High.

### C2. Labour
