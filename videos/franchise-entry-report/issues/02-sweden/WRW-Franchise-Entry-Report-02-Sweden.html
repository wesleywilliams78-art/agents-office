So what?

Fast food is 17.5% of sales but grew 5.6% in volume, while lunch and evening restaurants, 46.3% of sales, fell 0.8%. The 6% takeaway VAT widens that gap, and the industry body says it disadvantages seated restaurants most. A quick, takeaway-friendly format has room. A labour-heavy seated format does not. 04WHITE-SPACE OPPORTUNITIES

The open space is takeaway-led, in cuisines the big chains have not taken

Four gaps, from our reading of the page 3 evidence. Three of the four rest on thin data, and each says so.

1

Takeaway-first fast food

GAP
Takeaway pays half the VAT of a seated meal until the end of 2027, and fast food is the segment growing fastest. The scaled players we found are burger and hot-dog chains, so other cuisines sold quickly for takeaway are open.
EVIDENCE
Fast food volume +5.6% and traffic-adjacent +1.7%, both H1 2026. Takeaway VAT 6% against 12% eating in. Sibylla was sold on 3 Sep 2026 to a buyer aiming for 200+ outlets. [S2, S12, S30]
SUITS
A brand with a quick, portable menu and a proven delivery or counter model.
ROUTE
Master or area partner with an existing multi-site operator.
MWindow: before takeaway VAT may return to 12% on 1 Jan 2028 (inference)
2

Chicken beyond KFC

GAP
KFC had 24 sites through one franchisee in Oct 2025, and the only challenger found is a German brand with one site, in Lund.
EVIDENCE
KFC 24 restaurants, Oct 2025. Loco Chicken opened in Lund in early Sep 2026 (one report). We did not prove Popeyes or Wingstop absent. [S13, S14, S67]
SUITS
A chicken brand with a distinct product and a strong operator partner.
ROUTE
Master franchise or area developer.
MKFC needed about a decade to reach 24 sites (press, assumed from its 2015 start)

Asian fast casual and sushi

Company-ownedHighHighSlowMAX owned 141 of its 147 Swedish sites in 2022. No foreign owned-route case found. Five Guys’ structure is unverified, and its European owner was reported in Nov 2025 to be seeking a buyer for 50% of the European business. [S17, S49]
Small brand

Few units, no overseas record

Use one Swedish multi-site operator who knows wages, licences and property. Plan a slow ramp: KFC took about a decade to reach 24 sites (assumed). [S13]

Proven brand

Working in one or two countries

Direct franchising is how Pinchos and Sibylla grew, but Sibylla’s franchisor was sold in Sep 2026 and Pinchos is reported to be heading for sale, so write your standards into the contract. [S40, S30]

Scaled brand

Multi-country system

Subway chose one Nordic master for four countries. Back a partner who can take Sweden and its neighbours, and watch the concentration risk. [S47]

Six risks for a foreign franchisor

Six risks, each sourced
Franchisor failure

Wayne’s Coffee Sweden AB, the franchisor, went bankrupt on 4 Jul 2026 with debts of at least SEK 10m after cafés could not pay rent. Another company will carry on the 58 cafés. [S34]

Operator failure

Pizza Hut’s operator filed for reconstruction in Nov 2025 and went bankrupt on 9 Sep 2026, closing its 18 remaining restaurants. [S25, S26]

Over-fast expansion

Brödernas grew from 2017 to about 70 to 75 restaurants, applied for reconstruction on 16 Oct 2024, and by 18 Mar 2025 had 33 subsidiaries bankrupt and 53 in reconstruction. [S23, S22]

Lease cost

Starbucks’ Nordic CEO blamed ‘far too expensive leases’ when only two Swedish cafés remained in 2021. This is a 2021 company quote and shows a pattern, not 2026 rents. [S38]

Seated-format weakness

Lunch and evening restaurants lost 0.8% of volume in H1 2026, and the industry body says the food VAT cut disadvantages them. Total volume is 1.2% below its 2022 peak. [S2]

Failure-prone sector

Hotels and restaurants were the most bankruptcy-exposed industry in 2025, at 2.8% of firms. Hotel and restaurant bankruptcies fell 13% in Q3 2026 (UC), the fourth quarterly fall in a row, but chains still fail. [S50, S51]

07YOUR FIRST 90 DAYS

The first 30 days: decide the format, then find the partner

1

Pick the format: a quick, takeaway-led menu that sells a full basket.

2

Run the conflict check on the country and on each category before you contact anyone.

3

Prepare the franchise disclosure document to the 8 areas in Lag (2006:484), with a Swedish lawyer.

4

Take trade mark advice on the EU, UK and Swedish routes before you file.

5

Rebuild the unit P&L at Swedish wages: about 1.54 times pay once costs are added.

6

Model both VAT rates: 6% takeaway, 12% eating in, and a return to 12% from 1 Jan 2028 (inference).

7

Shortlist 3 to 5 Nordic multi-brand operators as master or area partners, and check each one’s balance sheet.

8

Ask each operator whether it applies the HRF and Visita agreement and how it plans for the 2027 talks.

9

Check licensing with the municipality for each target site, including alcohol.

10

Map local supply for meat, dairy and halal, and take advice on the rules we could not verify.

WHAT WE’D CHECK NEXT
  • Is the format takeaway-led, not seated?
  • Does the unit P&L hold at 54% on-costs?
  • Does it still work at 12% VAT from 2028?
  • Is the partner’s balance sheet sound?
  • Are your standards written into the contract?
