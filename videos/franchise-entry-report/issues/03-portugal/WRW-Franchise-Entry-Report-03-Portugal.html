04WHITE-SPACE OPPORTUNITIES

The open ground is Portugal’s new customer, and Brazil’s brands got there first

One big shift and three category gaps. Every entry we traced came through a local partner, and each of these will need one too.

1

Built for the new Portugal

GAP
The customer is changing fast: one resident in seven is foreign and tourists stay all year. Brazil’s franchisors are moving on it; few other foreign brands have said so.
EVIDENCE
14% of residents foreign, about a third Brazilian. Seasonality 36.4%, the lowest since 2013. 72 Brazilian franchise brands here, their No. 1 market abroad. [S1, S12, S66, S65]
SUITS
Brands whose offer travels across nationalities, and Brazilian brands with a language edge.
ROUTE
An Iberian or Portuguese multi-brand operator with room in its pipeline.
Lnational, Lisbon, Porto and the Algarve first
2

A second chicken brand

GAP
KFC is the only chicken chain at scale, and its nearest rival has not arrived.
EVIDENCE
KFC 81 sites (May 2026), up about 25 since end 2022 (Estimate). Popeyes missed 2022 and 2025. No Wingstop. [S14, S19]
SUITS
A chicken brand proven in two or more countries, with a simple kitchen and a takeaway menu.
ROUTE
Master or area development with a Portuguese multi-brand operator.
Mnational roll-out, one scaled rival
3

Bakery-café beyond Lisbon

GAP
The one scaled padaria chain is almost all in Greater Lisbon.
EVIDENCE
A Padaria Portuguesa: 78 of 84 shops in Greater Lisbon, 6 in the north (Aug 2025); a new Spanish owner since 2025. Porto-area rents run well below Lisbon’s. [S11, S41, S42]
SUITS
A bakery-café brand with central production.
ROUTE

“
If I had a brand, I wouldn’t build for the Portugal in the guidebooks. I’d build for the one that’s arriving, with an operator who has room for me.

05REGULATION AND COST TO OPERATE

No franchise law to clear; payroll and a 13% meal rate shape the numbers

No disclosure or registration duty. The costs sit in payroll and VAT, and the islands have their own rates.

06ENTRY ROUTE AND RISKS

Signing is easy in Portugal; opening is not

Pret signed for 70 Iberian sites in 2022 and has one Portuguese shop open; Popeyes has missed two launch dates; Five Guys missed 2025 while looking for sites. 07YOUR FIRST 90 DAYS

The first 90 days: price the payroll, pick the partner, then the region

1

Clear the brand: EU trade mark (€850) or INPI (€148.71).

2

Rebuild the unit P&L on 14 pay instalments: about €146 per €100 of monthly pay.

3

Price at 13% VAT on meals either way, 23% on drinks served.

4

Model the thin case: a 3.0% net margin.

5

Shortlist 3 to 5 Portuguese or Iberian multi-brand operators.

6

Check each partner’s brands for rivals before the first call.

7

Pick a first region: Greater Lisbon, the Porto area or interior towns.

8

Decide your customer: long-standing locals, new residents or year-round visitors. Then walk the sites.

9

Take tax advice on royalties (5% under the UK treaty) and the initial fee.

10

Set up the company online (from €220) and plan for the 2027 pay round.

WHAT WE’D CHECK NEXT
  • Is your category Rising on two signals, or Thin?
  • Does the P&L hold at €146 per €100 of pay and 13% VAT?
  • Does your partner already run a rival?
  • Have you modelled falling real sales?
  • Has a Portuguese lawyer checked the contract and royalty route?
