# Portugal, Issue 03: the En Route recap (Wednesday, 11 Nov 2026)

This is the Wednesday video for Franchise Entry Report Issue 03, built on 7 Nov 2026 exactly as Sweden's (`../../02-sweden/en-route/`): the same code, timing, grade, 1440p pipeline and music. It posts on LinkedIn and Instagram on Wed 11 Nov. The Portugal pill recap in `../recap/` (Edition 03) is not the Wednesday deliverable.

**The style** is Sweden's En Route look, in Portugal's colours:
- real Portuguese footage in rounded film-gate windows on ink;
- PORTUGAL in giant pale-green capitals (`#9FD3AE`);
- handwritten place names in Pristina, with coordinates (Lisbon, Porto);
- a dotted route line whose dot springs from chapter to chapter;
- an inked passport ENTRY stamp in Portugal green;
- ridge tides for every ground change;
- a filmic grade: grain 0.07, gate weave, flicker and light leaks.

**Colour (Wesley, 7 Nov 2026):** Portugal green `#046A38` leads: the chapter 02 ground, the ENTRY stamp, the kicker and wage chip on the bone page. Its tints `#2E8B57` and `#9FD3AE`; the pale tint carries the giant word and the accents on ink, because deep green does not read on dark footage. WRW red `#FF3131` appears only on ink (the MISSED stamp, the falling bars). Never green on green, red on red or red on green. No flag, armillary sphere or shield.

**Spec:**
- 60 s, 30 fps with 2 blended subframes.
- Sizes: 2560×1440, 1440×2560, 1440×1800, 1440×1440.
- Music: take 13 (as Sweden), re-mastered over the Portugal effects bed, about −14 LUFS.

## Build
1. Download the footage in `LICENCE.md` into `output/library/video/portugal-en-route/footage/`.
2. `./proxies-1440.sh`: graded, enhanced, all-intra 30 fps proxies, landscape at 3840×2160 and portrait at 1440×2560.
3. `python -I score.py audio.wav fx.wav`, then the master command above.
4. `node stills.mjs <W> <H> <dir> 0,1,…,59` for the stills gate.
5. `./render-1440.sh` writes `out1440/WRW-Portugal-En-Route-<size>.mp4` and `-effects-only.mp4`. `ONLY="2560 1440 16x9" ./render-1440.sh` renders one size.
6. Covers: frame 0 of each size, `out1440/WRW-Portugal-En-Route-cover-<size>.png`.
7. `npm run media` moves `proxies1440/`, `out1440/`, `stills/` and `music-samples/` to D: behind junctions.

`proxies1440/`, `out1440/`, `stills/` and `music-samples/` are rebuildable and not committed.
