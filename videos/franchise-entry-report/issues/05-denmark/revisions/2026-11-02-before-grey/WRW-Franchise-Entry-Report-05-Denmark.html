So what?

Margins are thin. Restaurants kept 5.9% operating profit in 2024 (Estimate) and spent 37% of turnover on staff. Only 6% of people who ate dinner the day before had a takeaway. A lean, high-volume model fits; a labour-heavy seated one does not. [S5, S15, S12]

IF I HAD A BRAND
“
If I had a brand, whatever the format and wherever the gap, I would start with the right franchise partner. Everything else comes after that.

Three Nordic markets, one lesson. In Denmark, KFC lost all 11 sites in 2025 when its one franchisee failed, and is coming back through an operator that already runs KFCs in Sweden. Pizza Hut’s Danish business went bankrupt in Nov 2025, a year after it returned. Norway and Sweden told the same story. [S16, S17, S32, S33]

So chase any gap on this page only with a partner who already runs a global brand in the Nordics, can carry 25% VAT and about DKK 206 an hour all-in, and will hold your standard. Write hygiene audits into the deal from day one.

UNDER 25 UNITS

A Nordic master partner

Choose an operator already running a global brand in the region. KFC's return and Subway's 2025 deal both went this way.

PROVEN IN 2+ COUNTRIES

Several country franchisees

Spread the risk. McDonald's uses 24 franchisees; KFC lost everything when its one franchisee failed.

CAPITAL TO SPARE

Own the first sites

Only with deep pockets. Domino's owned 27 stores and began closing them four years later.

Six risks

Six risks, each sourced
Hygiene

One DR documentary led to four sour smileys and closed all 11 KFC sites. Domino's earlier went into receivership after a TV exposé. [S16, S17, S31]

Demand

Confidence fell to −17.2 in October, and people expect unemployment to rise. Domino's left citing a small market. [S8, S31]

Margins

Restaurants made 5.9% operating profit in 2024 (Estimate). Lagkagehuset grew sales 8.2% and still lost DKK 76.8m. [S5, S39, S40]

Failures

Restaurant bankruptcies more than doubled in H1 2023, then fell 26% in Jan–Apr 2026. Restaurants were 11% of active-company bankruptcies in Jan–Sep 2025 but 5% of bankrupt turnover. October data out 5 Nov. [S73, S74, S75, S76]

Wages and hours

A floor of DKK 148.49 an hour plus about 39% on top (Estimate; 28% without the Thin pension). Restaurants close at midnight without a permit. [S70, S67, S56]

Same malls

Five Guys, KFC and Taco Bell all chose Field's or Fisketorvet. Our reading, not a measured fact. [S26, S17, S41]

07YOUR FIRST 90 DAYS

The first 90 days: find the partner, then the site

1

Pick the category from page 3: burger and chicken have the evidence.

2

Rebuild the unit P&L at Danish wages, with about 39% on top of pay.

3

Price at 25% VAT for eating in and takeaway alike.

4

Shortlist 3 to 5 Nordic multi-brand operators as master partners.

5

File an EU trade mark: a UK mark does not cover Denmark.

6

Walk Field's and Fisketorvet before taking a site beside every 2026 entrant.

7

Write hygiene audits into the agreement: one smiley story can close a brand.

8

Check closing hours and whether your sites need an extended-hours permit.

9

Check halal supply: stunning is compulsory.

10

Diary 1 Mar 2027: the wage floor steps up again.

WHAT WE’D CHECK NEXT
  • Does your partner already run a global brand in the Nordics?
  • Does the P&L work at about DKK 206 an hour all-in?
  • Does the price work at 25% VAT?
  • Is your trade mark filed at EU level?
  • Could every site pass a smiley inspection on day one?
