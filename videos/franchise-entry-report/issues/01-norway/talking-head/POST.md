KFC is coming to Norway. That's the best news an international chicken brand could get.

A KFC franchisee has agreed to buy Fly Chicken, Norway's own chicken brand. It grew from 11 sites to 19, most of them are converting to KFC, and the first openings are expected in early 2027.

Now look at who else is there. No Popeyes. No Wingstop. Nobody.

KFC is about to take the first rung of the ladder, and nobody holds the second.

Here's how I read it as an operator:
→ Labour in Norway runs at roughly £21 an hour all-in, so you need volume. Chicken gives you volume.
→ KFC is about to spend serious money teaching Norway how to eat branded chicken.
→ The number two in the market gets that education for free.

If I ran an international chicken brand right now, I'd be in a Norwegian master franchise conversation this quarter.
