## The verdict
Open, but not easy. Burgers are taken and legacy pizza is retreating. Chicken is the live window, and it narrows once KFC arrives, expected from 2027.

## Headline numbers
- 5.64m people, among Europe's highest incomes
- Restaurant prices 40% above the EU average
- No franchise-specific law or disclosure rules
- Hospitality wage floor NOK 204.79 an hour; about £21 an hour all-in (estimate)

## The four white spaces
1. **The second chicken brand** (L): the window is before KFC scales, 2027–28.
2. **Roadside and forecourt QSR** (M).
3. **Fast casual beyond Oslo** (M).
4. **Bubble tea and treat drinks** (S, thin evidence).

## The "If I had a brand" line
"If I ran an international chicken brand right now, I'd be in a Norwegian master-franchise conversation this quarter."
