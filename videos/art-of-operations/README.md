# Art of Operations

The Instagram reel series built on Sun Tzu's maxims.

- `masters/title` and `masters/vertical`: the two master compositions every clip started from.
- `clips/clip-NN`: one HyperFrames project per reel. `clip-01-v2` and `clip-03-v2` are revised takes kept next to the originals.
- `delivered/reels-revised`: the finished, approved MP4s (`Art of Operations - Reel N.mp4`) and the grid wall image. Copies of these also go to Wesley's Desktop folder after he approves each one.

Colourway and format decisions are in the assistant's memory (instagram-reel-format, reel-delivery-workflow). Moved from the old flat layout on 24 Oct 2026: `aoo-clip-NN` is now `clips/clip-NN`.
