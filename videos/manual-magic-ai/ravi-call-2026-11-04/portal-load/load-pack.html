2. Settings → Business Details

What does your business do?
Patisserie Penelope is a premium patisserie and café brand founded in London in 2014, now trading from 14 stores in the UK. Every store bakes its cakes, pastries, viennoiserie and bread fresh each morning from our own recipes, finishes them in front of guests and serves them with speciality coffee, teas and cold drinks. Guests eat in, take away, or order celebration cakes for collection and delivery. Our signature products are the raspberry and pistachio tart, the almond croissant and the flat white. We are now franchising internationally: our first franchised stores open in Dubai in 2027 and Mumbai in 2028, under master franchise agreements for the UAE and India. Every franchised store must deliver the same products, the same standards and the same guest experience as our London stores, adapted to local food safety law, local ingredients where approved, and local guest expectations.
What best describes your business model?
Franchising. Company-owned flagship stores in the UK, unit franchises in the UK, and master franchise agreements for the UAE and India, where the master franchisee opens and runs its own stores and sub-franchises others. The franchisor sets the recipes, brand standards, approved suppliers and training; franchisees run the stores day to day and are audited against the Operations Manual.
What best describes your business sector?
Food and beverage: premium patisserie and café, bakery made on site, eat-in and takeaway, with celebration cakes made to order.

How do customers primarily engage with you? (three boxes)

Box 1
In store, eat-in: guests order at the counter and we bring food and drinks to the table.
Box 2
Takeaway and click-and-collect: counter takeaway, pre-orders through our app, and delivery through third-party apps.
Box 3
Celebration cakes: ordered in store, by phone or online at least 48 hours ahead, with personalised decoration, for collection or delivery.

Which standard roles exist at a typical unit? (three boxes)

Box 1
Store Manager and Assistant Store Manager: run the store, the team, stock, cash and compliance.
Box 2
Shift Supervisor and Pastry Chef: lead each shift, bake and finish the daily range to recipe.
Box 3
Barista, Front of House Team Member and Kitchen Porter: make drinks, serve guests, keep the store clean.

Which regulated/brand standards apply to your business? (three boxes)

Box 1
Food safety and hygiene law in each country: the Food Standards Agency (UK), the Dubai Municipality Food Code (UAE) and FSSAI (India), with a certified Person in Charge on every shift.
Box 2
Allergen law and labelling: the 14 major allergens, our allergen matrix and a personal allergen handover for every order.
Box 3
Health and safety, fire safety and licensing, plus Patisserie Penelope brand standards checked by unannounced audits and mystery guest visits (minimum score 85%).

What equipment/systems are critical to a shift? (three boxes)

Box 1
Two-group espresso machine and on-demand grinders.
Box 2
Deck oven, combi oven, proofer, retarder, blast chiller and chilled display cabinets.
Box 3
EPOS and card machines, probe thermometers and the digital food-safety log.

3. Settings → Target Reader

Who is your target reader?
Primary – Store Manager of a franchised store (50%). Secondary – Shift Supervisors and Pastry Chefs (30%), and new Team Members in their first 30 days (20%). Franchisees and the master franchisee also read the Business Development, Growth & Profit category.
What jobs/outcomes must this reader achieve?
Open by 07:30 and close safely every day. Make every product to recipe, portion and presentation standard. Zero allergen incidents. Top food hygiene rating in every country (5 in the UK, Grade A in Dubai, compliant FSSAI inspections in India). Mystery guest average of 85% or more. Food cost at or below 28% and labour within budget. Training records complete for every Team Member within their first five shifts.
When/where will readers use this manual?
Pre-shift briefings on the store tablet. At the point of work on a phone, through QR codes on the espresso machine, ovens, hand-wash sinks and display cabinets. During induction in the first week. A printed copy of the emergency procedures in the back office. Wi-Fi can be patchy in back-of-house areas, so procedures must be short and quick to load.
What is the reader's baseline capability?
Mixed, multinational teams. For many Team Members in Dubai and Mumbai, English is a second language (Hindi, Arabic, Tagalog, Malayalam and Urdu are common). Many are new to premium patisserie and speciality coffee. Most are confident with smartphones but not with long documents. Store Managers hold a Level 3 food hygiene certificate or a Person in Charge certificate and have run a café or quick-service restaurant before.
