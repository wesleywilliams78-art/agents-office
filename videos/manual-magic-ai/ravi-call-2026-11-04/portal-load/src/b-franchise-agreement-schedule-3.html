Schedule 3: Franchisee Operating Obligations

This Schedule forms part of the Unit Franchise Agreement between Patisserie Penelope Franchising Limited (the Franchisor) and the Franchisee. Words with capitals have the meaning given in Clause 1 of the Agreement.

Part A: Operating the Store

3.1 Operations Manual. The Franchisee shall operate the Store strictly in accordance with the Operations Manual as amended by the Franchisor from time to time, and shall ensure every member of staff has access to the current version.

3.2 Trading hours. The Store shall open no later than 07:30 and close no earlier than 18:00, seven days a week, unless the Franchisor agrees otherwise in writing.

3.3 Products and recipes. The Franchisee shall sell only Approved Products, made to the Franchisor's recipes, portion sizes and presentation standards, using ingredients from Approved Suppliers only.

3.4 Food safety. The Franchisee shall comply with all food safety law in the Territory (including the Dubai Municipality Food Code or the Food Safety and Standards Act 2006 of India, as applicable), keep daily temperature and cleaning records, and hold at least one certified Person in Charge on every shift.

3.5 Allergens. The Franchisee shall keep the Franchisor's allergen matrix current at all times and follow the Franchisor's allergen handover procedure for every order.

3.6 Pest control. The Franchisee shall keep a contract with an approved pest-control provider, with inspections at least monthly, and keep the reports on site for 24 months.

3.7 Equipment maintenance. The Franchisee shall maintain all equipment in good working order, service the espresso machine and ovens every six months, and record each service.

3.8 Cash handling. All takings shall be counted by two people, recorded on the EPOS and banked within one business day.

Part B: People

3.9 Training. The Franchisee and the Store Manager shall complete the Franchisor's Initial Training Programme before opening. Every new staff member shall complete induction within their first five shifts, and training records shall be kept for every person employed.

3.10 Right to work and contracts. The Franchisee shall verify every employee's right to work in the Territory and issue a written contract before the first shift.

3.11 Uniform and presentation. All staff shall wear the approved uniform and follow the grooming standards in the Operations Manual.

3.12 Health and safety. The Franchisee shall carry out and record a fire risk assessment and a health and safety risk assessment before opening and every 12 months, and record every accident or near miss.

Part C: Brand, Marketing and Reporting

3.13 Brand standards. The Franchisee shall use the Marks only as set out in the Brand Guidelines and shall not alter signage, menus or packaging without written approval.

3.14 Marketing contribution. The Franchisee shall pay 2% of Gross Sales monthly to the Marketing Fund and spend at least 1% of Gross Sales on approved local marketing.

3.15 Guest feedback. The Franchisee shall respond to every online review and guest complaint within 48 hours, using the Franchisor's guest recovery procedure.

3.16 Audits and mystery guest. The Franchisee shall permit unannounced brand-standards audits and mystery guest visits, and shall maintain an average mystery guest score of at least 85%.

3.17 Reporting. The Franchisee shall submit weekly sales and a monthly profit and loss statement in the Franchisor's format by the 10th day of the following month.

3.18 Insurance. The Franchisee shall maintain public liability, product liability, employer's liability and property insurance, and provide certificates every year.
