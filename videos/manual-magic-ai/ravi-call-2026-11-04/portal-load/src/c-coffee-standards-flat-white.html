Coffee Standards: the Flat White

For every Barista. Our most popular drink: a double ristretto with silky, thin micro-foam, served in a 6 oz cup.

Recipe

ItemQuantityStandard
House espresso blend18 g groundFine grind, dialled in each morning
Ristretto (double)30 g out22 to 26 seconds
Whole milk (or oat on request)120 mlSteamed to 60°C to 65°C
Cup6 oz ceramicPre-warmed on the machine

Method

  1. Grind. Purge the grinder for one second, then grind 18 g into a clean, dry portafilter.
  2. Dose and level. Tap once and level with the distribution tool. No coffee on the rim.
  3. Tamp. Tamp once, level, with firm even pressure. Wipe the rim.
  4. Extract. Flush the group head for two seconds, lock in and start the shot straight away. Stop at 30 g. If the time is outside 22 to 26 seconds, adjust the grind and tell the Shift Supervisor.
  5. Steam. Purge the steam wand. Stretch the milk for two to three seconds only, then roll it until the jug is too hot to hold (60°C to 65°C). Wipe and purge the wand.
  6. Pour. Swirl the milk until glossy. Pour from height into the centre of the crema, then lower the jug and finish with a simple heart.
  7. Serve. Within 30 seconds of pouring, on a saucer with a spoon, handle at four o'clock.
Allergen note: milk. Oat milk contains oats (gluten). Ask the guest which milk they want and say the allergen at handover.

Quality checks

  • Crema is hazelnut brown with no white spots or holes.
  • Micro-foam is no more than 5 mm deep. Large bubbles mean the milk was over-stretched: remake it.
  • Never re-steam milk. Pour away what is left and rinse the jug.
