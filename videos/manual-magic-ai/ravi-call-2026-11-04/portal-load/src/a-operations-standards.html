Store Operations Standards

For Store Managers, Shift Supervisors and Pastry Chefs in every Patisserie Penelope store, company-owned and franchised.

1. Opening the store

The doors open at 07:30. The opening shift starts at 06:00 with a minimum of three people: the Shift Supervisor, a Pastry Chef and a Barista. Nobody opens alone.

If the store cannot open safely by 07:30 (for example a fridge above 8°C or no hot water), it stays closed. Call the Store Manager. Never open without hot running water.

2. Hand-washing and personal hygiene

Wash hands at the start of every shift, after a break, after using the toilet, after handling raw egg or waste, after touching your face, hair or phone, and between handling allergen-free orders and anything else.

  1. Use the hand-wash sink only. Never wash hands in the food-prep sink.
  2. Wet hands with warm running water.
  3. Apply one pump of liquid soap.
  4. Rub for at least 20 seconds: palms, backs of hands, between fingers, thumbs, fingertips and wrists.
  5. Rinse thoroughly.
  6. Dry with a single-use paper towel. Turn off the tap with the towel.
  7. Put the towel in the pedal bin.

If soap, towels or hot water run out, stop food handling and tell the Shift Supervisor straight away. Restock from the back-of-house store; the minimum stock is two days.

4. Allergen handling and handover

  • Every product has an allergen card listing the 14 major allergens. The allergen matrix in the back office is the master copy; the Store Manager updates it the same day a recipe changes.
  • Ask every guest ordering food: "Does anyone in your party have an allergy we should know about?"
  • If a guest declares an allergy: tell the Shift Supervisor, wash hands, use clean tongs and a clean plate, and take the item from a sealed or freshly opened tray.
  • The person who prepared the order hands it over in person and names the allergen: "This is your raspberry tart, made without nuts."
  • If you are not sure, do not guess. Say so and offer a pre-packed, labelled alternative.

5. Closing the store

  1. 17:45: last orders for hot drinks. Stop baking. Move the day's unsold items to the reduced shelf or the donation box.
  2. 18:00: lock the door. Record closing temperatures.
  3. Clean and back-flush the espresso machine. Empty and wash the grinder hopper on Fridays.
  4. Clean all surfaces, display cabinets and the floor to the cleaning schedule.
  5. Cash up: count the takings with a second person, bag, record and place in the safe.
  6. Take waste and recycling out. Check the toilets, back door and windows.
  7. Set the alarm. Two people leave together.
