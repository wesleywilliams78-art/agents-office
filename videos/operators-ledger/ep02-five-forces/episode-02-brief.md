# Episode 02 Brief — Porter's Five Forces
**Series:** The Operator's Ledger · **Plan row:** 02 · **Monday:** 2 Nov 2026 · **Working title:** "Five forces taking a cut of every site"
**Module:** Strategy · **Source:** Michael E. Porter, "How Competitive Forces Shape Strategy", *Harvard Business Review*, 1979; revisited in "The Five Competitive Forces That Shape Strategy", *HBR*, 2008.

## The angle
On an MBA, the five forces judge how much profit a whole industry lets its players keep. This episode runs them on one site's street instead, and reads each force as a line on that site's P&L. It builds on Episode 01: the five forces are where SITE 10's money went.

## Numbers check
- **SITE 10, from Episode 01:**
  - Sales £13,000.
  - Food £3,900 (30%), labour £5,200 (40%), rent £2,600 (20%), running £1,600 (12%).
  - Food + labour + rent = 90% of sales, which is "ninety pence in every pound".
  - The bar uses 0.04 px per £.
- **The order:** a £20 order at 30% commission = £6, leaving £14, before food and labour.
- **The scorecard (illustrative):**
  - Street A: 3 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 3 = 12.
  - Street B: 5 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 = 21.

## Sources
- **The framework:** Porter (1979, 2008), as above. Labour as a supplier follows Porter's own treatment of labour as a supplier group. That was checked against secondary summaries; confirm the exact wording against the 1979 article before quoting him directly.
- **Delivery-app commission:** UK rates are commonly reported at about 14% (restaurant delivers) to about 30% (app delivers), and are negotiated per restaurant. The script says "up to six pounds on a twenty pound order" (30%), labelled illustrative. Sources include [Whito UK takeaway marketing costs](https://whito.co.uk/takeaways/research/uk-takeaway-marketing-costs/), [Yorkshire Post](https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/read-this/heres-how-much-it-costs-a-business-when-you-order-food-via-uber-eats-deliveroo-and-just-eat-3128207) and [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/the-hidden-costs-of-the-food-delivery-revolution-the-surprising-premium-added-to-your-next-takeaway-a9kAZ8K7lB30).
