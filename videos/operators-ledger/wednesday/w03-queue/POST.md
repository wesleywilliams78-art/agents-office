> Here's the count I'd give any site manager. Two counts at 12:10, ten minutes, one site:
>
> 1. Count the orders in the queue at ten past twelve
> 2. Count what the till serves in the next five minutes, and divide by five
> 3. Divide the queue by that: it's how long the last person waits
> 4. Tally the walk-aways and price them at your average lunch spend
> 5. Divide the queue by the wait you'd accept: that's the speed you need at noon
>
> The fix is rarely more staff all day. It's one more serve a minute between twelve and one.
