# The Operator's Toolkit — Wednesdays

One tool every Wednesday, from Wesley's personal profile, pointing back to Monday's episode on the WRW Hospitality page. The row for each week (tool, gated version, keyword) is in `../EPISODES.md`.

## The model
| Tier | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Free | The complete tool for **one site**, built on Monday's topic and numbers | The carousel (PDF document post) |
| Gated | The **multi-site version**: every site side by side, ranked and flagged | Comment the keyword, then the form, then the download |
| Paid | Putting it all together across their sites, with Wesley | A conversation |

## Each week's folder (`wNN-<slug>/`)
- `carousel/`: the HyperFrames source, eight slides at 1080 × 1350, one slide per second. Snapshot each slide, then save as PDF.
- `slides/`: the eight PNGs and a contact sheet.
- `WRW-Toolkit-NN-…-one-site.pdf`: the free carousel to attach.
- `WRW-Toolkit-NN-…xlsx` (or similar): the gated multi-site file, with live formulas, an example row and a legend.
- `POST.md`: the post, the reply to keyword comments, and the follow-up for leads with 3+ sites.
- `FORM.md`: the form wording, privacy line and confirmation message.

## Rules
- The tool is specific, detailed and executable, built on that Monday's topic. Never generic, never a crippled half-tool.
- The numbers come from the episode (SITE 1 and SITE 10) and reconcile, labelled illustrative.
- The keyword is one closing line; the post leads with the free value.
- The post names WRW Hospitality twice and has no link placeholders or "check out the link here": Wesley schedules it as written and doesn't come back to it. Point back by name ("…over on the WRW Hospitality main feed"). Only the keyword reply carries a link (the form).
- Slide 1 is the cover and thumbnail, a finished poster. The last slide carries the keyword, the pointer to Monday's episode and the WRW sign-off.
- Every file is branded and carries the copyright line: "© 2026 WRW Hospitality (Wesley Williams). Free for use in your own business. Not to be resold, rebranded or redistributed." Spreadsheets follow Wesley's branded w01 sheet. Each tab has the red title band and logo, the warm background, the sign-off, the © line and a © footer, with protected formulas and copyright in the file properties. `wrw_brand.py` does this. The carousel PDF has the © line on its last slide.
- The office drafts; Wesley posts, replies and sends.
