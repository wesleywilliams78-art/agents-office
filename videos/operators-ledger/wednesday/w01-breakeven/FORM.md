# Wednesday 1 — the gate form

**Purpose:** deliver the multi-site spreadsheet automatically, and tell you who runs how many sites.
**Simplest set-up (free):** a Google Form, with the spreadsheet in Google Drive set to "anyone with the link can view". The form's confirmation message shows the download link, so no email tool is needed. A Tally or Typeform form works the same way.
**File to share:** `WRW Toolkit 01 - Break even for every site.xlsx`

---

## Form title
**Break-even for every site: the multi-site version**

## Description (shown at the top)
> The spreadsheet from this week's Operator's Toolkit. Put in each site's weekly sales and costs; it works out every site's break-even, its daily number for the manager, and ranks your sites by how exposed each one is.
>
> Three quick questions, then it's yours.

## Questions
1. **First name** (short answer, required)
2. **Email** (short answer, required, validated as email)
3. **How many sites do you run?** (multiple choice, required)
   - 1
   - 2–3
   - 4–10
   - 11–25
   - More than 25
   - Not yet: planning my first or second site
4. **What kind of business?** (multiple choice, optional)
   - Café or coffee
   - Restaurant
   - Bar or pub
   - Quick service or takeaway
   - Franchise (as franchisor)
   - Franchise (as franchisee)
   - Other
5. **Can WRW Hospitality email you the next Operator's Toolkit and the occasional note?** (checkbox, optional, unticked by default)
   - Yes, email me. I can unsubscribe at any time.

## Privacy line (under the questions)
> WRW Hospitality uses your details to send you this spreadsheet and, only if you tick the box above, future toolkits. We don't share them. Ask us to delete them at any time: [your contact email].

## Confirmation message (after submit)
> Here it is: [Drive link to the spreadsheet]
>
> Start with the yellow cells on the Sites tab. Rows 6 and 7 are the two sites from Monday's episode, so overwrite them with yours. The Rank column tells you which site to look at first.
>
> If a number surprises you, message Wesley Williams on LinkedIn.

---

## What to do with the answers
- **3 or more sites ("4–10" and up, or "2–3" with a franchise):** a warm lead. Send the follow-up message in `POST.md` within 48 hours.
- **Ticked the email box:** add them to the toolkit mailing list. **Didn't tick it:** send nothing beyond this spreadsheet. That's what UK GDPR and PECR expect.
- **Each week, track:** comments with the keyword, form fills, how many run 3+ sites, conversations started, calls booked.
