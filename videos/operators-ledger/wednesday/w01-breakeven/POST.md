## The post (Wesley's final, ready to schedule)

> Your first site made money. Your tenth might not, and the P&L won't tell you until it's too late. 🤔
>
> On Monday, WRW Hospitality Operator's Ledger showed why: rent and the core team don't fall when sales do. Site ten needed £13,520 a week to break even and was taking £13,000.
>
> Here's the worksheet I'd give any site manager. Four steps, one site, ten minutes:
>
> 1. Add up the costs that don't move (rent, the core rota)
> 2. Find the share of each £1 that does (food, packaging, card fees)
> 3. Work out what each £1 keeps
> 4. Divide one by the other, then by your trading days
>
> That last number, the daily break-even, is the one to give your manager.
