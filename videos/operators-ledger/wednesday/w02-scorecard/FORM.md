# Wednesday 2 — the gate form

**Purpose:** deliver the multi-site scorecard automatically, and tell you who runs how many sites and who is weighing a lease.
**Simplest set-up (free):** duplicate the Wednesday 1 Google Form, change the wording below, and put the spreadsheet in Google Drive set to "anyone with the link can view". The confirmation message shows the download link, so no email tool is needed.
**File to share:** `WRW-Toolkit-02-Scorecard-for-every-site.xlsx`

---

## Form title
**Street scorecard for every site: the multi-site version**

## Description (shown at the top)
> The spreadsheet from this week's Operator's Toolkit. Score every site and every lease you're weighing on the five forces, with a scoring guide for each one. It totals and ranks each street, names the force that hurts most, shows the cushion over break-even, and says what to do next.
>
> Four quick questions, then it's yours.

## Questions
1. **First name** (short answer, required)
2. **Email** (short answer, required, validated as email)
3. **How many sites do you run?** (multiple choice, required)
   - 1
   - 2–3
   - 4–10
   - 11–25
   - More than 25
   - Not yet: planning my first or second site
4. **Are you weighing a new site or lease right now?** (multiple choice, optional)
   - Yes, in the next 3 months
   - Yes, later this year
   - Not right now
5. **What kind of business?** (multiple choice, optional)
   - Café or coffee
   - Restaurant
   - Bar or pub
   - Quick service or takeaway
   - Franchise (as franchisor)
   - Franchise (as franchisee)
   - Other
6. **Can WRW Hospitality email you the next Operator's Toolkit and the occasional note?** (checkbox, optional, unticked by default)
   - Yes, email me. I can unsubscribe at any time.

## Privacy line (under the questions)
> WRW Hospitality uses your details to send you this spreadsheet and, only if you tick the box above, future toolkits. We don't share them. Ask us to delete them at any time: [your contact email].

## Confirmation message (after submit)
> Here it is: [Drive link to the spreadsheet]
>
> Start with the Scoring guide tab, then fill the yellow cells on the Scorecard tab. Rows 6 and 7 are Street A and Street B from Monday's episode, so overwrite them with yours. Score your existing sites too: the Group tab shows which force is costing the whole business.
>
> If a score surprises you, message Wesley Williams on LinkedIn.

---

## What to do with the answers
- **3 or more sites ("4–10" and up, or "2–3" with a franchise):** a warm lead. Send the follow-up in `POST.md` within 48 hours.
- **Weighing a lease in the next 3 months:** the warmest lead of the week, whatever the site count. Follow up first.
- **Ticked the email box:** add them to the toolkit mailing list. **Didn't tick it:** send nothing beyond this spreadsheet. That's what UK GDPR and PECR expect.
- **Each week, track:** comments with the keyword, form fills, how many run 3+ sites, how many are weighing a lease, conversations started, calls booked.
