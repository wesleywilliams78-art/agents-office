## The puzzle
One operations manager. Six sites and the week holds. Eleven sites and it is firefighting. Span of control is the tool: how many people (here, sites) one manager can really run. The answer is not a number of sites; it is the hours left in the week after a site goes wrong. The verdict is a hiring call: the second area manager arrives at site seven. Season question: Athena's road from 1 site to 10 needs a second area manager before site seven.

## Visual idea: the hour shelf (the hero is the week, not a café)
Ep 03 hero: a café. Ep 04: a street of two cafés. Ep 05: **the manager's week as a physical shelf of 45 one-hour cubes**, with the cafés as small 3D blocks standing behind it. The week itself is the object the viewer learns to read; the sum becomes a thing you can see overflow.

New devices (beat or reuse the library in `40-Marketing/operators-ledger-look.md`; none repeats unchanged):
1. **Hour shelf (running device).** 45 slots on an ink rail. Scene 4: twelve cubes turn warm grey (the fixed admin), thirty-three stay open.
2. **Site pieces.** Each site is a 4-cube piece (three solid, one thin for the road) that drops onto the rail with a spring and a tick. Six fit with nine empty. Eleven are dropped onto the same rail: the last pieces slide off the end and fall to the floor (the unvisited sites; red).
3. **The 45 built from cubes.** The hook's numeral builds from hour cubes, then the cubes tip down into the shelf.
4. **Trouble block.** One site flashes red and an 8-cube slab drops on the shelf: it fits at six sites (one slot left), and shoves four pieces off at eleven.
5. **Camera.** A dolly along the rail counting the hours; a top-down for the packing; a low whip to the floor for the fallen pieces; a slow orbit in the verdict. Reuse the roll kick from Ep 04.
6. **Hire = a second shelf.** In the verdict a second rail slides in below the first and the eleven sites split six and five, each with spare hours (a red frame on the unhired version).
7. **Sound.** A soft tick per cube, a phone buzz on the trouble block, from the Openverse SFX bank via Earshot. Bed as Ep 04.

Rules deliberately bent (name them in the build note): the hero is an abstract object rather than a café; labels sit on the cubes (hours counted on the piece itself); camera moves in every scene; a fall (the only gravity move in the series so far).

## Numbers check (all illustrative, weekly; assumed inputs marked)
- Week 45 hours (assumed). Fixed work: reports, payroll sign-offs, head-office meetings 12 hours (assumed). Free = 45 − 12 = **33**.
- Per site, about 4 hours (assumed): 3 on site + 1 travel and write-up.
- 6 sites: 6 × 4 = **24**; 33 − 24 = **9 spare**.
- 11 sites: 11 × 4 = **44**; 33 − 44 = **−11, eleven short**.
- One site in trouble: **8 hours** (assumed). Six sites: 9 − 8 = **1 spare**. Eleven sites: 33 − 8 = 25 free; 25 ÷ 4 = 6.25, so 6 visited and **5 unvisited**.
- Limit with one site in trouble: (33 − 8) ÷ 4 = 6.25 → **6**. Two in trouble: (33 − 16) ÷ 4 = 4.25 → **4**.
- Visit cut to 3 hours: (33 − 8) ÷ 3 = 8.33 → **8**.
- Same figures spoken: forty-five, twelve, thirty-three, four (three plus one), twenty-four, nine, forty-four, eleven short, eight, one, five, six, four, three, eight.
