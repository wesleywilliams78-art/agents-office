Same menu. Same brand. One site is packed at 7am. The other fills up on Saturday morning.

Who is each one for?

If you can't say, both sites end up making the same offer to everyone.

Episode 4 of The Operator's Ledger works it through with STP: segmentation, targeting, positioning.

1. Segment: split the people around a site by who they are and when they come.
2. Target: pick the group you serve better than anyone nearby.
3. Position: promise them one thing.

In the example, a station site takes £11,000 of its £20,000 a week from the 7am commuter: "served before the train". A residential site takes £5,720 of its £13,000 from Saturday brunch: "nobody hurries you". Same menu, a different promise at each.

Illustrative figures, weekly.

Your question for Monday: which customers bring each site the most sales?
