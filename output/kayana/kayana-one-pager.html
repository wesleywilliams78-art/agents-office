Kayana reseller proposal

What Kayana is offering, what it could earn, where we stand, and what has to happen before anything is signed.
4 November 2026
For Wesley Williams and Yousif
Prepared by WRW Hospitality
Position: pursue with conditions. Take the London walkthrough, settle the disclosure wording, and get Matt’s answers in writing. Sign nothing and recommend Kayana to no client until the conditions in this page are met.

01The offer

Matt at Kayana, by email on 4 Nov 2026. We earn a share of what each client we introduce spends, once the sale is made.

StreamOur sharePaid
Hardware30% of hardware revenueOnce
Software30% of the monthly software feeMonthly
Card processing50% of the margin over Kayana’s base rateMonthly

Matt’s examples: base 0.3%, client charged 0.6%, we take 0.15% of card turnover; base 0.4%, we take 0.10%. Onboarding: agreement and NDA, a KYC form, then our own CRM of clients and commission.

02What Kayana is

One supplier and one bill for ordering, payments and operations, aimed at smaller multi-site brands. Its own deck (v1.5) claims:

  • Kiosks, EPOS, QR, web and app ordering, kitchen screens and card payments, with multi-site admin: central menus, pricing and reporting, local control.
  • Contracts from 12 months. The client owns the hardware. Installed in two weeks, live within 72 hours, support around the clock.
  • Markets: UK and Ireland (largest), Spain, Netherlands, Portugal, Australia, US, Canada. UAE online payments only. Italy soon.

All of these are Kayana’s claims, unchecked. The deck gives no prices and names no franchise client.

05The risks that matter

  • Independence. Our value is unbiased advice. A commission can undermine it, or look as if it does. Written disclosure first, and a vendor-neutral comparison every time.
  • The NDA. It must not stop us telling clients about the commission. If it does, we cannot sign it as drafted.
  • Reach. Clients outside Kayana’s markets cannot be referred.
  • Regulation. Whether introducing payment services needs any registration is unchecked.
  • Reputation. If Kayana fails a client we introduced, it reflects on us.

06Ask Matt before signing

  • Any exclusivity, or bar on recommending rival systems?
  • Termination, and does commission continue on clients already introduced?
  • Clawback on churn, refunds or disputes? How long does each share last?
  • Is “profitability” before or after scheme and processor costs, and is the base rate fixed?
  • Who owns and supports the client; what are their contract length and exit fees?
  • When and how is commission paid? Please send the price list, the processor behind payments, and a UK client reference.
