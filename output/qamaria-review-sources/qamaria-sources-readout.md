Prepared 9 Nov 2026. Read-only summary of the split source files in this folder. Headings and field names only; no client content copied.

## How this was read
- The gap analysis (2 parts) was read in full, line by line.
- The two long manuals (10 + 8 parts, about 800 KB) are plain text with no markup. I read their structure with a script (numbered headings, chapter titles with delivery tags, and the repeated field names) and spot-read the opening chapters and the ends of both final parts. I did not read every paragraph of body text. Sub-headings in the narrative chapters were picked up by a short-line rule, so a few may be missing or extra.
- Client names, places and tool names in headings are replaced with [client], [city], [country], [tool]; the owner's name in tags is shown as "owner".
- Missing or cut-off files: none missing. All 20 parts are present. Parts end part-way through sentences where the size split fell (normal). Creamy part 10 ends cleanly on "End of Changelog". Imran's part 8 ends on the list of regulatory references with no end marker, so I cannot confirm it is the true end.

## Field key used below
- [A] = the standard five fields: Introduction / Minimum Operating Standards / Roles & Responsibilities / Measure, Analyse, Improve / Tools & Resources
- [B] = the "needs input" fields: Why this chapter matters / What you need to provide / Easiest way to get this done
- Other per-chapter fields seen: What's needed to write this SOP / How to get this done (tool-referenced chapters).
- Neither manual has separate fields called question list, evidence needed, owner, check or sign-off. Closest equivalents: "What you need to provide" (the input asked for), "Measure, Analyse, Improve" (the check), "Roles & Responsibilities" (owner), and the delivery tag in the chapter title (status/sign-off).
- Tags in titles (Creamy): YSF ADVISORY (drafted, with a sub-status such as pending review, final draft, completed from voice note or notes), NEEDS OWNER INPUT, TOOL-REFERENCED.
